# Sample photos

These photos appear *inside the editor* in the product screenshots on
photovibe.mory.dev. They are not part of the Photovibe application and are not
shipped with it — they exist only so the screenshots show a real photo instead
of a blank canvas.

All three come from Unsplash and are used under the

## Replacing them with your own photos

Drop your own JPEGs in this folder using the same three filenames, then
re-run the capture from the `site/` directory:

```bash
pnpm capture
```

Every screenshot on the site is regenerated from the live editor.
